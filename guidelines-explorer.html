Exploring the AI Lang Guidelines

The Guidelines for the use of AI in Language Education were developed in the AI Lang project of the European Centre for Modern Languages. They rest on four principles: AI-assisted language education should be safe, responsible, purposeful and human-centred. Each principle is put into practice through two guidelines, eight in all. Each guideline is described through competences, written as questions, and each competence comes with reflection questions.

The Guidelines are a reflective tool, not a compliance checklist. The reflection questions have no correct answers. They are meant to help you think about your own teaching, alone, with colleagues, or with learners.

What you will do

  1. Orient. Read your guideline and sum up, in one sentence, what it asks of a language teacher.
  2. Engage. Choose one competence and work through its reflection questions for a teaching situation one of you knows. Mark the question you found hardest and the one you found most helpful.
  3. Try. Test one point with a general-purpose chatbot.
  4. Prepare. Note something hard, something helpful and a question for the plenary.
