Artificial intelligence for language education

In an age of artificial intelligence, the AI Lang project helps teachers keep language education grounded on a set of fundamental ethical values: safety, social responsibility, pedagogical purpose and human-centredness.

The AI Lang Guidelines

The Guidelines for the ethical use of artificial intelligence in language education is a formative framework that helps language teachers, teacher educators, administrators and policy makers involved in language education develop contextually appropriate ways to use AI to support teaching and learning.

This resource consists of an overview of existing guidance, a framework of eight guidelines (operationalised in competences and supported with reflection questions) and usage cases illustrating the guidelines in practice.

The AI Lang Course

The AI toolbox and playground is a structured course that helps language teachers learn and develop their skills and confidence in using AI tools in their day-to-day teaching practice.

The course covers the theoretical foundations of AI for language education, practical guidance on using different tools, as well as examples of AI-supported activities and lessons. Its modular structure means that teachers can directly access the resources that are most relevant to their interests, or work through the course in a more systematic way.
